# Privacy Policy — Packet AI

_Last updated: 4 November 2026_

Packet AI ("the app") is a personal AI assistant that runs **entirely on your
device**. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

## Summary

- The app has **no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking**.
- Your chats, notes, voice notes, imported documents and saved memories are
  stored **only on your device** and are never uploaded by the app.
- The developer **does not collect, receive, sell or share** any personal data.

## Information processed on your device

| Data | How it is used | Where it is stored |
|---|---|---|
| Chat messages and AI replies | Generating answers with the on-device AI model | Local database on your phone |
| Microphone audio | Converted to text on-device when you use voice input or record a voice note | Audio is not kept; the transcribed text is stored locally if you save it |
| Notes, tasks, memories ("remember …") | Shown in the app and given to the on-device AI so it can answer about them | Local database on your phone |
| Documents you import (PDF, DOCX, TXT, MD) | Text is extracted so you can search and ask questions about it | Local database on your phone |
| App settings | Theme, language, avatar and assistant preferences | Local database on your phone |

All of this data is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
You can also delete conversations, notes, documents and tasks inside the app.

## Microphone permission

The app asks for the microphone permission only so you can talk to the
assistant and record voice notes. Speech recognition runs on your device. Audio
is never sent to the developer or to any server.

## Spoken replies

Replies are spoken either by a voice model downloaded into the app or by
your phone's own text-to-speech engine (for example Google Speech
Services). The text of the reply is passed to that engine on your device
to create the audio. Installed voices run offline. If your speech engine
is set to use online voices, it may process the text under its own
provider's privacy policy.

## Internet use

The app uses the internet **only to download AI model files** (the language
model, speech recognition, voices and optional 3D avatars) when you choose to
download them. These files come from public hosting services:

- Hugging Face (`huggingface.co`)
- GitHub (`raw.githubusercontent.com`)

As with any download, these services receive standard technical information
such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies. The app does not
send them any of your chats, notes, documents or audio. Once the models are
downloaded, the assistant works without an internet connection.

## Reporting AI responses

Each AI reply has a **Report response** button. If you use it, the app opens
your own email app with a pre-filled message to the developer containing the
reported reply. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email. Reports
are used only to improve the app's safety and quality.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect
data from anyone.

## Security

Your data stays inside the app's private storage on your device, which other
apps cannot access. Keep your device protected with a screen lock.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page
